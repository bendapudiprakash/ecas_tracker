💡 Zerodha Console Unitized NAV Methodology Explained

Standard portfolio valuation line charts spike falsely whenever fresh capital is deposited into a demat account or mutual fund. Zerodha Console's Unitized NAV methodology eliminates cashflow noise by creating a unitized index initialized at 100.00 in Month 0. In each subsequent month, the NAV compounds strictly by the organic price return of matched holdings: NAV_t = NAV_(t-1) * (1 + r_t). This reflects your true investment compounding rate.
